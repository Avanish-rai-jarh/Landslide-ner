ABOUT LANDSLIDEGUARD

AI-based landslide monitoring for North Eastern India.

LandslideGuard is a prototype system that combines environmental data, live rainfall information and machine learning to estimate landslide susceptibility at selected locations across the North Eastern Region of India.

●
NER MONITORING PROTOTYPE
01 / PURPOSE

Turning environmental signals into understandable risk information.

Landslide risk can depend on multiple environmental characteristics. LandslideGuard brings these signals together into a single interface so that users can explore a location, view its environmental context and inspect the model's susceptibility assessment.

The current prototype is specifically designed for the North Eastern Region of India and covers Arunachal Pradesh, Assam, Manipur, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagaland, Sikkim and Tripura.

03 / PROTOTYPE STATUS
!
IMPORTANT

Prototype, not an operational emergency-warning service.

LandslideGuard currently provides an AI-based susceptibility assessment together with environmental and live rainfall information. The prototype should not be interpreted as an official emergency alert system.

Operational early warning would require further validation, calibrated uncertainty, validated rainfall and soil-moisture thresholds, forecasting capability and reliable alert-delivery infrastructure.

PROJECT DIRECTION

From susceptibility mapping toward informed monitoring.

The prototype provides a foundation for combining machine learning and environmental monitoring into a location-focused landslide information system for the North Eastern Region.
