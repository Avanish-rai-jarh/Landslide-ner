IMPORTANT

Understanding the output.

LandslideGuard is a prototype monitoring and research system. Its susceptibility result should be considered together with current environmental conditions and local ground information. Rainfall indicators shown by the prototype are intended for monitoring and are not validated emergency trigger thresholds.
